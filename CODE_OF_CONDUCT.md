# Code of conduct

This is a friendly place to work on a hard problem. In short:

- Be patient with newcomers and assume good faith.
- Keep the discussion about the code.
- Respect anonymity. Don't try to find out who someone is behind their handle, and never post anyone's personal details.
- No harassment of any kind.
- No game files, and no links to them. See [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block people who don't follow this. To report a problem, message a maintainer on [Discord](https://discord.gg/E65kyCNgSS).
